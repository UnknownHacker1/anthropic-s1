# Anthropic's S-1, in charts

An independent visual read of the S-1 that Anthropic, PBC filed with the SEC on October 26, 2026. Every chart links to the page in the filing it came from.

Live: https://unknownhacker1.github.io/anthropic-s1/

## What's in it

- Quarterly revenue, Q1 2025 to Q2 2026
- Compute cost per dollar of revenue
- The Q2 2026 operating profit (the first of the six quarters reported)
- The H1 2026 net loss, split into the business and the fair-value accounting
- Operating costs vs revenue by quarter
- Customers paying $100K+ a year
- Price per preferred share, Series C to H
- Operating cash flow
- Revenue model and cloud-marketplace share
- The compute and infrastructure deals
- Who controls Anthropic: the five share classes, the Long-Term Benefit Trust, the board
- Lines in the filing worth reading

## How the numbers are checked

- Every figure is copied from the filing, with its printed page number.
- Sums are cross-checked against each other: cost lines against totals, quarters against the half-year and full-year statements, and the loss waterfall against reported net loss.
- Every "jump to source" phrase was checked to appear verbatim in the filing.
- Where a number is my own arithmetic rather than a printed figure, the page says so.

Plain HTML, CSS and JS with hand-drawn SVG charts, no build step, no tracking.
